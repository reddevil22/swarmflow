# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, no harassment or discriminatory
behavior, and keep discussions focused on the work. Maintainers may remove
comments, commits, or contributions that violate these expectations and will
communicate reasons when doing so.

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private security advisory
channel (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)); reports are handled confidentially.
